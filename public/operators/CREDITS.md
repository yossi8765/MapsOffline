# Operator logos

Small logos of public-transport operators, keyed by the GTFS `agency_name` in `index.json`.
All files come from Wikimedia Commons and were minified with scour (paths and colours unchanged).
The logos are trademarks of their owners and are used only to identify the operator of a line.
